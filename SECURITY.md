# Security

Screenshots sees everything on your screen, can record your microphone and system audio, and keeps a history of captures on disk. A bug in any of these can expose someone's data, so please report it privately.

**How:** open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/ciro-maciel/screenshots/security/advisories/new) on this repository. Please don't open a public issue for it.

**What helps:** the macOS version, the steps, and what was captured, kept or exposed.

You will get an answer within a week. Once a fix ships, the advisory is published with credit to you, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

Supported: the latest release only.
